This software can change power, brightness, color, color temperature, effects,
and automated behavior. Any third-party control or modification carries risk.
Incorrect, unsupported, interrupted, or excessive operations may cause
unexpected behavior, loss of settings, network disconnection, an unresponsive
or inoperable controller, reduced service life, physical device damage, or
other loss.

Use is at your own risk. Stop using the software if the device becomes unusually
hot, unstable, unresponsive, visibly damaged, or behaves unexpectedly. Remove
power only when it is safe and consistent with the manufacturer's instructions,
and contact Nanoleaf Support for device-specific assistance.
